## What Doorsly Is

Doorsly is property management software designed for landlords with small and medium portfolios.

It keeps everything in one place:

* Properties and tenants
* Rent tracking
* Maintenance
* Messages
* Expenses
* Documents

Dora helps you do the work faster.

This is an early version and we're actively improving it.

---

## Why Doorsly Exists

Most landlords manage properties across:

* Spreadsheets
* Emails
* Text messages
* Notes
* Bank statements

Doorsly brings it together into one system.

Dora makes it faster to use.
